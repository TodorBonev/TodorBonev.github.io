Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 2026

What We Collect

Numilith does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

All game progress (level scores, stars, settings) is stored only on your device using your device's local storage. This data never leaves your device and is never sent to any server.

Third-Party Services

Numilith does not use any advertising networks, analytics services, or third-party SDKs that collect data.

Permissions

Numilith declares the INTERNET permission required by the Android WebView engine it runs on. This permission is not used to transmit any data — the app operates fully offline after installation. No camera, microphone, location, or contacts access is requested.

Children

Numilith is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no personal data, the app complies with COPPA and GDPR-K requirements by default.

Changes

If this policy changes in a future version, the updated policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
