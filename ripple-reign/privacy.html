Privacy Policy: Ripple Reign

Effective date: October 8, 2026

Ripple Reign ("the Game") is a mobile game for Android published by Virevon. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Virevon. This policy explains what the Game stores on your device, which data leaves it, who receives it and what you can do about it. We wrote it for version 1.0.0 and later.

In short: the Game has no accounts, no in-app purchases, and we do not sell your data. Your progress stays on your phone. The only data that leaves it comes from Google's advertising and Firebase services, which are described below, and where the law requires it you are asked for consent first.

1. Who the Game is for

The Game is meant for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the Game, write to us (section 12) and we will help; there is nothing we can delete on our side, but we can tell you how to remove the data held by Google. Where the age of digital consent in your country is higher than 13, please ask a parent or guardian before you play.

2. What stays on your device

The Game keeps its progress in a single file named save.json in the app's private storage on your phone. It contains your stars and coins, which cosmetics you own and have equipped, your best Endless score, your Daily Pond result, your settings (volume, vibration), which tutorial hints you have seen, and how many times you started each level. This file is never uploaded by us, it holds no name, e-mail address or identifier, and no other app can read it. Uninstalling the Game deletes it.

3. Accounts, purchases and what we do not collect

  • There is no account, sign-in or registration in the Game.
  • The Game has no in-app purchases. Coins are earned only by playing and by watching an optional rewarded ad.
  • We do not ask for or collect your name, e-mail address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera.
  • We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with anyone except the service providers named in this policy.

4. Consent (EEA, United Kingdom and similar regions)

When you start the Game for the first time in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform appears before any advertising or analytics starts. You can choose Consent, Do not consent or Manage options (to choose purposes and partners one by one). Elsewhere the form is shown only if local law requires it.

The Game follows your answer with Google Consent Mode v2. Your choice is translated into the four Google consent signals ad_storage, analytics_storage, ad_user_data and ad_personalization, and each one is switched on only if you allowed the matching purposes:

  • If you do not consent, Firebase Analytics collects nothing: no events are logged and no analytics identifier is kept. Ads may still be offered, but not personalised with your data.
  • If you consent, ads can be personalised and Analytics runs as described in section 6.
  • Until you have answered, nothing is collected.

How to change your mind at any time: open the Game, go to Settings and tap PRIVACY SETTINGS (this button appears wherever a consent choice applies to you). The form opens again and your new choice takes effect immediately. Withdrawing consent also clears the analytics data held in the app on your phone. Withdrawing does not affect what was processed before.

5. Advertising: Google AdMob

The Game shows one optional rewarded video ad (you choose to watch it to earn coins). There are no ads while you play and no banners. Ads come from Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited, Google LLC). To serve and measure ads, AdMob may receive:

  • your advertising ID (an Android identifier you can reset or delete, see section 8), your IP address (from which a rough location such as country or region is derived), device and app information (model, operating system, language), and
  • how you interact with ads (requests, impressions, clicks, completion), and diagnostic information about the ad SDK.

Google and its advertising partners use this to show ads, limit fraud, count impressions and (only with your consent where consent is required) personalise ads. See Google's policy and how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

6. Analytics: Firebase Analytics

If consent is not required for you, or you have given it, the Game uses Google Firebase Analytics to learn how the Game is played, for example which levels are too hard. The Game sends only game data:

  • level_start: mode (campaign, daily or endless), level number, attempt number;
  • round_end: mode, level, how the round ended, duration, win or loss, stars, your share of the pond;
  • tutorial_step: which tutorial hint was shown, completed or skipped;

plus the events Firebase records on its own (session start, first open, app updates) and technical data: an anonymous app-instance ID, the advertising ID (when allowed), approximate location derived from the IP address, device model, operating system, app version, language and the install source. We do not send names, e-mail addresses, user IDs, free text or any other personal data, and we do not use Google signals or link Analytics to advertising accounts.

Retention: Analytics data is kept for up to 2 months after your most recent activity in the game.

7. Crash reports: Firebase Crashlytics

The Game uses Firebase Crashlytics to find and fix crashes and freezes. When the Game crashes, a report is sent to Google containing the stack trace, the state of the app at that moment, device details (model, operating system version, memory, free storage), the app version and a random installation identifier. It contains no account data and is not used for advertising or marketing.

Crashlytics stays on even if you decline analytics or advertising consent, because crash reports are not usage analytics or advertising and they are needed to keep the Game working for everybody. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in the stability and security of the Game (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Crash reports are kept for 90 days. You cannot switch Crashlytics off inside the Game; if you do not want it, please do not install the Game, or write to us (section 12).

8. Your choices and rights

  • Consent: change or withdraw it any time with Settings → PRIVACY SETTINGS (section 4).
  • Reset or delete the advertising ID: in Android go to Settings → Privacy → Ads (on some phones Settings → Google → Ads) and tap Delete advertising ID (or Reset advertising ID). You can also turn off ad personalisation there. Resetting gives apps a new ID that cannot be linked to the old one.
  • Delete your local data: uninstall the Game (or clear its storage in Android settings). This removes save.json.
  • Vibration and sound: both can be switched off in the Game's Settings.
  • Your legal rights: depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or UK GDPR) you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your data, to object to processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Because the Game keeps no account and sends no name or e-mail address, we normally cannot tie data held by Google to you; to exercise a right, write to us (section 12) and we will explain what is possible, or use Google's privacy controls.
  • California and other US states: you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your data for personalised ads at any time with Settings → PRIVACY SETTINGS (section 9).

9. US state privacy rights

Under some US state privacy laws (for example in California, Colorado, Connecticut and Texas), sharing data with an advertising partner so that you see personalised ads can count as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. In the Game this applies to the data described in section 5, which Google AdMob may receive to show you ads. We do not sell your personal data for money.

If you live in a US state with such a law, you can opt out at any time: open the Game, go to Settings → PRIVACY SETTINGS and choose Don't sell or share my data. You can change your choice there whenever you like. We will not treat you differently because you opted out. To ask about any other right you have under your state's law, write to us (section 12).

10. Who receives data and where it goes

Data leaves your phone only to our service providers: Google (AdMob, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Installations and the consent platform). They act on our behalf for Analytics and Crashlytics and as independent controllers for advertising. Everything is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Google may process data in countries outside your own, including the United States, under the safeguards described in Google's terms (such as standard contractual clauses and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework).

11. Changes to this policy

If the Game starts to handle data in a new way (for example a new SDK or a new feature) we will update this page and its effective date before the new version is released, and ask for consent again where needed.

12. Contact

Developer and responsible party: Virevon. Questions about privacy, or requests about your data: quantumesqy@gmail.com
